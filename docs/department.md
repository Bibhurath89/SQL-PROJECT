# Department Definitions

## Accounting

Department number: 10

Department name: ACCOUNTING

Accounting is responsible for financial reporting and accounting operations.

## Research

Department number: 20

Department name: RESEARCH

Research is responsible for research and technical development.

## Sales

Department number: 30

Department name: SALES

Sales is responsible for customer-facing sales activities.

When a user refers to "Sales employees", filter using:

dept.deptno = 30

or

emp.deptno = 30

depending on the query structure.

## Operations

Department number: 40

Department name: OPERATIONS

Operations handles operational activities.

## Human Resources

Department number: 50

Department name: HR

HR handles employee and organizational processes.
